1. Introduction

Flipbook Maker ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is dedicated to safeguarding user information. This document details how information is collected, processed, and protected in compliance with the GDPR, CCPA, and Microsoft Store Developer Policies.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Email address, optional profile name, and encrypted password hashes.

  • Anonymous Readership Metrics: Aggregated document view counts, page turns, and session duration to provide analytics to document creators.

3. Cloud Storage & Data Security

All converted publication assets, vector canvas pages, and PDF files are securely hosted on Cloudflare R2 Object Storage with encrypted transmission (TLS/HTTPS) and encrypted storage at rest. We do not sell or share user data with third-party advertisers.

4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

All subscription payments (Free, Professional, Business) are handled exclusively and securely via the Microsoft Store Commerce Platform. We do not collect or store credit card details.
